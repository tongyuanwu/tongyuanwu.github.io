## Workshop overview

This hands-on workshop covered spatial data processing, remote-sensing analysis, and visualization using Python and Google Earth Engine.

Topics included:

- raster manipulation with Rasterio;
- Google Earth Engine image collections and indices such as NDVI;
- land-cover classification with CART and Random Forest models;
- spatial visualization with Matplotlib for publication-quality figures.
